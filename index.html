Who is Gael?

Gael is a slave knight — one of the oldest and most expendable warriors in the Age of Fire. Slave knights were used as cannon fodder, sent ahead of armies into dangerous, cursed territories that would kill regular soldiers. They were built to be disposable. Despite this, Gael was fiercely loyal and devoted.
